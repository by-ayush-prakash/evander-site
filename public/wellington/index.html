What the demonstration will test

  • Propose an action

    Wellington proposes deleting a spreadsheet that looks like an old draft.

  • Check permissions

    Evander checks whether Wellington has permission to delete the file.

  • Trace dependencies

    The workspace model connects the spreadsheet to Monday’s operations report.

  • Explain the decision

    Evander would pause the deletion and show why it needs review.

  • Test what changes

    The pilot would change a relevant fact and test what happens when evidence is missing.

  • Measure time spent checking

    We plan to measure how much time people spend verifying and correcting Wellington’s work.
